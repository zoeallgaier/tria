## Zoe's answers (2026-09-14)

Asked and answered, so these are settled:

- **Who can message whom.** Mutual friends message freely. A follower of a
  PUBLIC account can send a message REQUEST, which the account accepts or
  denies. Nobody else can start a chat. A denial is quiet, like a declined friend
  request.
- **Groups** can be started by anyone, with their friends. Activity chats are
  the other source of groups.
- **A message** carries text, a photo or a GIF (the comment bar's picker), can be
  hearted, and can reply to a message. No read receipts, no typing indicator.
- **Unread** is a dot on the Chats tab and a bold row. Never a count.
- **Live** while a chat is open (Supabase Realtime), a push per message, and a
  mute per chat.
- **Chats replaces Updates as a tab.** Updates becomes a pinned row at the top of
  the chat list (TikTok's inbox) and opens its own page, filter and all. The tab
  wears a chat icon. The Chats page has the filter disc and, beside it, a glass
  **new chat** button.
- **Report, block, leave and delete** in every chat. Messages are guarded by RLS,
  not end-to-end encrypted. Messages keep forever.
- **Public site.** A signed-out visitor on the WEB can open a public post or a
  public profile by link, and can browse Discover. Place is hidden from them on a
  public activity. Comments are readable, not writable. The app keeps its gate.
- **Activities.** RSVP is Going / Maybe / Can't go. A hand-picked activity's
  chat holds everyone invited; a circle or public activity's chat holds whoever
  says Going or Maybe. From the chat and from the activity you can change your
  own RSVP, and the host can add or remove invitees.
- **Calendar** is a SUBSCRIBABLE feed ("amazing"), so a phone's calendar updates
  itself as you RSVP. Add to calendar stays, and moves into the reaction bar.
  (Moved back into the ••• on 2026-09-16, Zoe: for cleanliness.)
- **No new activity fields.**
- **Week-of cards** sit on top of My Circle: activities you are GOING to in the
  next seven days, stacked, each dismissable.
- **An activity's chat archives three days after the activity** and stays
  readable.
- **Order:** Chats first (Activities need them), then Activities, then the
  public site.

## Calls Claude made that Zoe did not (veto any of them)

- **The Chats filter** narrows to All / Direct / Groups / Activities. Updates
  keeps its own All / Mentions filter on its page.
- **Archived activity chats** sit behind one "Archived" row at the foot of the
  list rather than mixed into it.
- **Search engines** are not invited in, and a shared link keeps Tria's one
  preview card. A per-post preview needs a server function; not in 1.7.
- **Activities posted before 1.7 get no chat.** Only new ones do.
- **Messaging someone** is a row in their profile's ••• sheet and in the friend
  tie menu, as well as the new chat page.

## Stage 1 · Chats

**Database: [`supabase/add-chats.sql`](../supabase/add-chats.sql). ZOE RUNS IT**,
in the SQL editor, and then redeploys the push function (its `messages` branch is
new). Until it runs, the Chats page shows only the Updates row and a line saying
chats aren't set up yet. Nothing else breaks.

- `chats` (direct / group / activity), `chat_members` (status member / request /
  denied, `last_read_at`, `muted`, `cleared_at`), `messages`, `message_hearts`.
- Every rule is in the database. `start_direct_chat` holds the who-may-message
  rule, `start_group_chat` and `add_chat_members` the friends-only rule,
  `can_send_message` the block gate and the archive. Clients never insert a chat
  or a member row themselves.
- A requester cannot see the other member row until it is a full member. That
  fence is what keeps a denial quiet.
- Realtime publishes the four tables; RLS still applies to what it forwards.

**Client.**

- `Store`: chats, members, messages and hearts load with the world and stay live
  on one Realtime channel while signed in. Every change goes through `write()`.
- Routes: `#/chats` (the tab), `#/chat/<id>`, `#/chats/new`, `#/chats/requests`,
  and `#/updates` kept as the pinned row's page. The Chats tab stays lit on all
  of them.
- The message bar IS the comment bar: same markup, same native `TriaPostBarPill`
  (kind `comment`), same photo tray. Nothing new in Swift for the bar.
- The tab's icon comes across the bridge as SVG markup (`tabSpec`), so the new
  glyph needs no Swift change either.

## Stage 2 · Activities

**Database: [`supabase/add-activity-chats.sql`](../supabase/add-activity-chats.sql).
ZOE RUNS IT, then redeploys the push function** (it gains the calendar feed and
the maybe / can't go wording). Until then: RSVPs stay yes-or-nothing (a Maybe
or Can't go tap says it couldn't save), no activity gets a chat, and the calendar
row says the calendar isn't set up yet.

- `headcount.status` (going / maybe / cant). Existing rows are going. The number
  on the card is still only the yeses (`Store.headcountFor`); `rsvpsFor` has all.
- A trigger on a new activity makes its chat. Members follow the plan: a
  hand-picked activity's invite list, or a circle / public activity's going and
  maybe answers. The host is always in. `add_chat_members` now lets the host
  invite (on a hand-picked activity that also adds them to the invite list) and
  `remove_chat_member` takes a guest off both.

  **Superseded in 1.8 by `supabase/chat-roster.sql`**, which widened both RPCs
  and is where the current rules live: ANY member may add (a guest can bring a
  friend, and being brought writes the invite-list row whatever the plan's
  audience, because the allowlist became a floor); removing is the host's on a
  plan and ANY member's in a group, which until then had no way to remove anyone
  at all. See "the allowlist is a floor" in [data.md](data.md), and
  `openChatPeopleSheet` for the one sheet that now does both.
- **The calendar feed rides the push function**, as a GET with
  `?calendar=<token>`, because that function already takes calls with no key and
  a calendar app cannot send one. Tokens live in `calendar_tokens`, readable only
  by their owner, not on `users`, which everyone can read.
- The page: Going / Maybe / Can't go above the guest list; maybes listed for
  everyone, noes for the host. The headcount glyph only shows: a link to that list
  in a feed, a switch to it on the page. It used to put you in with one tap,
  which filed a Maybe or a Can't go as Going (fixed 2026-09-16). The chat's ••• has Your RSVP, and for the host
  Invite friends and Remove from activity under People.
- Add to calendar was a glyph beside the headcount, and left the •••. It went
  back into the ••• on 2026-09-16 (Zoe, "for cleanliness"): the row is the same
  four glyphs on every card again. A quote's ••• offers the plan it points at.
- Week-of cards on My Circle (`paintWeekCards`): hosting or going, next seven
  days, soonest first, dismissable per device, with a way into the chat.
- An activity chat archives three days after its day: the Archived shelf at the
  foot of Chats, no bar, a line saying it can still be read.
- **An activity's comments and its chat stay two things.** Tried and taken out
  the same day (Zoe, 2026-09-14: "a bad call"): the post page showing the chat in
  the comments' place, with activity chat links redirecting there. The page keeps
  its comments, and the chat keeps its own page. What survived it is
  `activity_chat_posts()` in the database and `Store.hasActivityChat`, which
  `setRsvp` uses to re-read chats straight after an answer.
- **A message is hearted with a double tap** (Zoe, 2026-09-14), with the like's
  sparkle; a hold opens its menu, which is what lets a tap answer at once. A
  double tap only adds; taking a heart back is in the menu.
- **Add to calendar only shows on activities that haven't happened yet**, the
  host's included. Asked about 2026-09-14: every activity visible signed out
  was from July.

## Stage 3 · Public site

Landed in the app 2026-09-14 (`renderGuest` in app.js). **The migration
`supabase/public-site.sql` has to be run** before signed-out Discover has any
authors to draw; check with the two PostgREST reads at its foot.

  posting or commenting) and on comments of any post anon can read.

  yours) is caught in capture phase and lands on `#/join` (`GUEST_ASKS`). No
  comment bar signed out; the thread reads.

  friend, the Discover row's Add, and the "Add them…" line under a private
  profile's lock are all left off for a guest; the row shows its → instead.

  a view or column grants.
- **The app keeps its gate.** `nativeShell()` never reaches the public site.
- About (web): a Get Tria on the App Store button, and the home-screen install
  fold is Android only. The iPhone/Android switch is gone.
- The wordmark is the logo (`icons/wordmark.svg`, a mask wearing the brand band), over an Oxygen slogan. It was lowercase Instrument Serif italic until 2026-09-16.
